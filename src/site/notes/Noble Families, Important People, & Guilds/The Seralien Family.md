The Seralien Family has been upheld as a prominent name in Palonia, brought to power through the champion ancestor Esper Seralien. The family became a rather famous name through the efforts of the late champion of Atia who was a masterful cleric, operating out of a small temple in Arcstead Estrye. Through her lifetime Esper worked to ensure her deity's name be spoken in reverence, that temples in her honor were cared for properly and made countless efforts to provide food to all who entered the temples. She worked to ensure her families legacy up until her death. Some say that due to her devotion to Atia, the goddess blessed her lineage, as since Esper took the mantel of champion her family has gone on to produce some of the strongest healers ever seen in history; since then most of Esper's descendants have had silver eyes and white hair. Though not a family of political importance, the Seralien family are very well known for their healing capabilities, and often times are the most sought after clerics and paladins in the world.

Due to how long the Seralien name has been around their family has grown quite large, and has been spread to different families as well. While they might not hold power in a political sense their name still carries much more weight than others, being connected to a Seralien is to be connected to real power so not many are allowed to get close. However their generosity and kindness are left unmatched by other families in power, often times a Seralien will be regarded as 'a light brightening a room'.
