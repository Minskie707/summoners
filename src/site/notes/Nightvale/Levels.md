# Nightvale Academy — Grade Levels

**[[Nightvale/Nightvale Academy\|Nightvale Academy]]** — the school’s year rankings are more than nomenclature; they are the visible arc of a summoner’s soul. Progression here is absolute: students do not “fail out” in the mundane sense. A letter of invitation is a covenant — you either fall in the pursuit of becoming a Summoner, or you rise and are granted the title. The Academy enforces this with iron ritual, mysterious consequence, and a social architecture that reshapes a pupil at every turn.

(Organization: Nightvale Academy)

---

## The Codex (schoolwide rules)

- **No ordinary failure:** Students never “graduate” to the street by academic failure; those who cannot meet the Academy’s standard leave by other, often hush-hush, means.
    
- **Titles during study:** Until graduation, a student is referred to by their year + [[Nightvale/Summoning/Summoning Specialties\|Summoning Specialties]] (for example: _Procyon Hunter_, _Mizar Warg_). On graduation they become simply _Summoner_.
    
- **Outside employment & contracts:** Students may not sign binding guild contracts; they may accept certain work under Academy supervision. [[Nightvale/The Nightvale Council\|The Nightvale Council]] reviews and sometimes approves off-campus engagements. Nightvale Council
    
- **Guild membership:** Honorary membership is possible (an Academy promise to sign on graduation). Forming a guild is allowed so long as Academy duties remain first priority.
    
- **Cultural note:** Nobility and powerful houses watch the Academy closely — patronage during the student years can decide future careers.
    
- **Uniforms**: Unlike previous years; students are required to wear their uniforms for events or jobs in which the school is being represented off campus — while on campus uniforms are required at all times however alterations are allowed.

## Procyon (Year 1 — Initiates)

**Role & tone:** Newborn summoners. Frightened, awed, raw.  
**Core goals:** Master basic summoning etiquette, stabilize the initial bond, and learn containment and safety rituals.  
**Curriculum highlights:** Summoning safety, call-and-bind basics, Ether hygiene, egg/incubation care (if assigned).  
**Restrictions:** Procyons are generally confined to Academy grounds for the year. Contact with the outside is restricted except for sanctioned holidays and ceremonies.  
**Rites & events:**

- _Egg Custody (optional):_ In some years Procyons are entrusted with an egg upon arrival — a test of protection, patience, and instinct. Failure to protect the egg has social and disciplinary consequences.
    
- _[[Events & Festivals/The Procyon Showcase\|The Procyon Showcase]]:_ A public demonstration of first-year promise presented to Palonia’s elite. This new Showcase is attended by Count [[Noble Families, Important People, & Guilds/Karken Spiros\|Karken Spiros]] and other patrons — it is a rare chance for sponsorship and employment prospects. Players must present creativity over raw power (the novice’s advantage: improvisation and roleplay). Count Karken Spiros
    

---

## Mizar (Year 2 — Bonded)

**Role & tone:** Students move beyond fear into practice. Bonds begin to solidify.  
**Core goals:** Form the first stable summoning bond and refine teamwork within a party.  
**Curriculum highlights:** Bond strengthening rituals, fieldcraft, summoning maintenance, negotiation with minor planar allies.  
**Privileges & limits:** Off-campus travel allowed, but Mizars cannot take public job postings without Academy mediation. They are not yet permitted to accept direct payment/contractual obligations.  
**Social notes:** This year often deepens friendships and party cohesion. Mizars have lighter coursework than upper years, so they can cultivate a summon and begin identity shaping.

---

## Polaris (Year 3 — Adepts)

**Role & tone:** Powers become visible; egos grow. The student is no longer a beginner.  
**Core goals:** Learn advanced summoning theory and enter structured external work.  
**Curriculum highlights:** Advanced ritual magic (not mundane spells — techniques for summoning interaction and control), ethics of binding, cross-plane contracts, limited mentoring by visiting deities or high-tier staff.  
**Privileges:** Polaris students may take approved jobs off campus with staff and **Nightvale Council** authorization. They may form or join guilds as _honorary members_ (signing deferred until graduation).  
**Rules & risks:** Job leave is conditional — failure to return or dereliction loses off-campus privileges. Contracts remain illegal until graduation.

---

## Vega (Year 4 — Challenged)

**Role & tone:** The school’s crucible. The first _true_ trials and heavier consequences.  
**Core goals:** Prove battlefield and ritual competence in sanctioned [[Nightvale/Challenges\|Challenges]]. Learn to coordinate complex summons under pressure.  
**Curriculum highlights:** Tactical summoner combat, endurance rituals, advanced containment, and ethics of summoner command.  
**Challenges:** Vega students face officially sanctioned Challenges — trials designed to push survival, strategy, and party cohesion. These are often dangerous; historically about half the cohort proceeds beyond this year.  
**Social notes:** Alignment with guilds and protective alliances intensifies; many students become inward-looking, prioritizing their parties.  

---

## Regulus (Year 5 — Masters-in-Practice)

**Role & tone:** Focus shifts from personal survival to showcasing readiness for Summoner rites.  
**Core goals:** Prepare for _[[Events & Festivals/The Summoners Gauntlet\|The Summoners Gauntlet]]_ and _[[Events & Festivals/The Summoning Games\|The Summoning Games]]_. Refine your summon’s development and synergy.  
**Curriculum highlights:** Team choreography, large-scale summoning maneuvers, mount/transport training (Aetherglide boards, mounts), and public spectacle preparation.  
**Rites & events:**

- _The Summoner’s Gauntlet_ — a solo or duo gauntlet designed to test a summoner’s personal skill and their summon’s capabilities.
    
- _The Summoning Games_ — an academy-wide tournament that tests party coordination and summon utility.  
    **Personal changes:** Many students become hardened; family relationships may strain, alliances polarize, and students increasingly specialize.
    

---

## Sirius (Year 6 — Finale)

**Role & tone:** The final crucible. Near-graduates stand on the edge of legend or oblivion.  
**Core goals:** Mastery and profession specialization. Final challenges intensify; the cohort is noticeably reduced.  
**Curriculum highlights:** Leadership in summoner command, diplomatic summoning (summoning in courts/war councils), final thesis (a culminating ritual or major public demonstration).  
**Privileges & outcomes:** Graduation can lead to positions on the **Nightvale Council**, high-level guild offers, or roles in courts across **[[World History/Palonia\|Palonia]]**. A few choose adventuring, others political life. Some use their final year to prepare for rule or to refurbish family houses. Palonia

## Events & Sample Mechanics (quick reference)

- **Procyon Showcase (Year 1):** Performance/Arcana/Deception/Animal Handling checks. Social rewards: patronage, small magical trinkets, or favor tokens.
    
- **Challenges (Years 4–6):** Multi-format tests; fatalities possible. Use variable CRs and nonlethal consequences. Consider saving throw consequences (long-term injuries, curses, or reputation loss).
    
- **Gauntlet & Games (Year 5):** Tournament structure: preliminary rounds, elimination, and judged finale. Scoring mixes objective success and style/performance.
