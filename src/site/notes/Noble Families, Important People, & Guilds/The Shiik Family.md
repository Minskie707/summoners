The Shiik Family is a prominent family from Vixgate, a quite family of engineering and innate magical power. The family itself gained much of its notoriety and fame in more recent years with its current heads, Clement and Aurora-Pnoma Shiik. Both of them have founded integral technology for the world of Palonia, with Aurora-Pnoma Shiik founding the Rose Guilds and many of the advancements made within them. Their most prominent contributions have been the Aetherglide boards, and the crystal prosthetics made by the Prism Collective. Together they brought forth a new age to technological development, the Aether Age, bringing forth new creations born of Ether and Crystal.

The couple met when Clement was set to join an expedition as a 4th year student at the Nightvale Academy, The Aurum Index of the Rose Guilds needed more people to take on a Purple Dungeon, the Academy volunteered a party of students and a teacher to join them. There Clement met Aurora-Pnoma, it was spring so her Eladrin features were in full bloom, to him she was the most beautiful creature he'd ever seen. Having been from Agorith Fostrela originally, where it was rare to see members of the fae species, Clement had found a curiosity in them. The dungeon took a week to complete, during that week the two found an interest in one another, and when they had completed the dungeon they decided to try to build something together. From there he signed a contract with her, to join her guild collective with his own guild, The Prism Collective. After graduation Clement did join the rose guilds as he had agreed to, however shortly before the couple was to be married, he began working for the Nightvale Council as well.

When Aurora-Pnoma became pregnant with their daughter, she chose to step down from her position in the rose guilds, it was meant to be temporary. Together the couple has built a grand legacy for their daughter, building a life where she should want for nothing and fear no individual. Their home sits near the heart of Vixgate, close to the Rose Guildhall, within the wealthier neighborhood.
