Liquid Starlight is an ancient magical component of mythical origins, a silvery glowing liquid in appearance. Its true origins are unknown, however many stories have been made over the years to try and explain the creation of such an extraordinary magical material. The material itself is very rare, most magic shops wont carry it, as of more recently there's only three reputable shops in all of Palonia that carry it. The shops are located within Vixgate Fostrela, Hythe The Shattered Isles(TSI), and Nagos Estrye.
