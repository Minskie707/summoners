The Seralien Family has been upheld as a prominent name in Palonia, brought to power through the champion ancestor Esper Seralien. The family became a rather famous name through the efforts of the late champion of Atia who was a masterful cleric, operating out of a small temple in Arcstead Estrye. Through her lifetime Esper worked to ensure her deity's name be spoken in reverence, that temples in her honor were cared for properly and made countless efforts to provide food to all who entered the temples. She worked to ensure her families legacy up until her death. Some say that due to her devotion to Atia, the goddess blessed her lineage, as since Esper took the mantel of champion her family has gone on to produce some of the strongest healers ever seen in history; since then most of Esper's descendants have had silver eyes and white hair. Though not a family of political importance, the Seralien family are very well known for their healing capabilities, and often times are the most sought after clerics and paladins in the world.

Due to how long the Seralien name has been around their family has grown quite large, and has been spread to different families as well. While they might not hold power in a political sense their name still carries much more weight than others, being connected to a Seralien is to be connected to real power so not many are allowed to get close. However their generosity and kindness are left unmatched by other families in power, often times a Seralien will be regarded as 'a light brightening a room'. 

In recent years many tragedies have befallen the Seralien family, only weeks after loosing the elder's of the Seralien line to old age the family also lost its current heads; leaving their eldest child Arvad as the head of the household. After losing their parents Lord Callahan Seralien and Lady Davina Seralien in a tragic fire at their estate in Arcstead, Arvad was immediately recognized as the living heir as Lord Callahan's siblings had passed years prior or disregarded the family name. At the age of 18 years he was tasked with taking care of his mass of siblings, the household, the business, as well as his young cousins. The community seemed to rally for them, offering support and aid where they could. Neighbors would bring meals, help with laundry and cleaning duties, and even assisting in the childcare for the younger ones.

As the years passed and Arvad got a handle on running his newfound estate their neighbors decided to draw back some to allow the family to grow; although their elderly neighbor still offers to watch the younger ones. Now at 22 years, Arvad mans the temple as the head priest, his younger siblings and cousins helping out where they can.

### Arvad Seralien

Sir Arvad Seralien, currently 22 years old and the youngest head priest to date. Although he was once set to become a member of the Queensguard, he abstained from the offer once his siblings were left to his care. A decision that never sat right with his sister Ena, she always argued that Arvad needed to think of himself for once. Arvad is a master Paladin of Atia, those who trained along side him hold him in high respect out of a primal fear of his abilities on the battlefield.

### Ena Seralien

Lady Ena Seralien, currently 21 years old and the eldest daughter of the Seralien name. Working in the temple she was trained by her mother from a young age until her death when Ena was 17, causing her to step back from her training. While she was on track to be one of the best clerics of the ages, losing her mother and mentor hurt her deeply and left her with too many unanswered questions. She had argued with her brother Arvad about taking over the temple, she believed they would be fine and that he was throwing away his opportunity to get out of their town and make something of himself; she never really got over that grudge and their relationship has suffered for it. Despite being upset with Arvad she enjoyed having the help with the younger siblings.

### Kamaria Seralien

Lady Kamaria Seralien, currently 19 years old; currently a student of Nightvale Academy Vega Level, specialty Saor. When her parents died at 15, she watched as her elder siblings rushed and pushed themselves in that first year; then her letter from the Academy arrived, she saw it as an answer to lift some of the burden on her siblings. Her sister Ena encouraged her to go, saying it would be a good opportunity and that she needed to gain independence somehow. In many ways Kamaria is much like their mother, Lady Davina, holding herself in a gentle graceful manor.

### Vidalia Seralien

Lady Vidalia Seralien, currently 18 years old; currently a student of Nightvale Academy Polaris Level, specialty Phalanx. At 14 she witnessed her family falling into ruin, her older siblings working hard to build back what had been lost when their parents died. A year after her sister who had always protected her left for school, Kamaria had always stuck up for Vidalia when they were young, she was often bullied for her frail stature and dark hair which contrasted her siblings drastically. Not long after her sister left, she herself received a letter of admission from the Academy, without much council from her family she had already decided on going, determination present in her every action.

### Corvin Seralien

Lord Corvin Seralien, currently 16 years old, he works at the temple with his elder siblings. Similarly to Vidalia, he is one of two children of the current Seralien line with black hair. He tends to keep to himself, even when he's working in the temple. Many of the townspeople have seen him training in the woods surrounding their estate. Despite his desperate attempts to remain invisible to the public eye, his tall muscular stature and angled features attract an abundance of unwanted attention. His twin brother Aaric does his best to sway the attention from his brother but the striking contrast of Corvin's black hair and silver eyes keeps them coming back. 

The twins were 12 when their parents passed and have little memory of the whole event, they had been at a neighbors house when the worst of it happened.

### Aaric Seralien

Lord Aaric Seralien, currently 16 years old, he's expected to join his sisters at Nightvale Academy this upcoming term. His eldest siblings Arvad and Ena keep telling him to go and get out of the town of fangirls, however he can't help but feel guilty for leaving Corvin to deal with his problem. Aaric had always been a bit of a flirt, though in the past few years it had gotten much more intense as he got older, this caused a lot of attention to be drawn to Aaric and by association his twin brother Corvin; as they always spent their time together when not working at the temple. He used to accompany Corvin in his training, however after people heard about them training, many came to watch the spectacle. In many ways Aaric looks exactly like his twin, they have similar heights and are both fairly muscular, however Aaric inherited the white hair like the rest of his siblings; save for Vidalia.

The twins were 12 when their parents passed and have little memory of the whole event, they had been at a neighbors house when the worst of it happened.

### Penelope Seralien

Lady Penelope Seralien, currently 15 years old, she's the youngest of Lord Callahan and Lady Davina's children. She received a letter from the Nightvale Academy at the same time as her brother Aaric, to the surprise of many of her siblings, as she is quite softspoken and always enamored with her books, the only one not surprised was Corvin, he always understood her in ways their other siblings couldn't. She was always working with the creatures and summons that would visit the temple from time to time, she'd always say "even they know a safe space to relax." As she's been preparing to leave for the academy she can't help but worry for her home, as if she feels something going wrong.

### Ezra Seralien

Lord Ezra Seralien, currently 18 years old, he helps his cousins at the temple and helps train Corvin and Aaric when he has free time. His parents died when he was 8 years old, from then on his siblings and him were left in the care of their Uncle Lord Callahan and their Aunt Lady Davina. When they died at 14 it was no more than a wave to him, he had been through it before and since then he had grown rather apathetic. He still holds on to his empathy for the sake of his siblings as they were too young to remember much of their parents.

### Hayden Seralien

Lord Hayden Seralien, currently 17 years old, he is currently a student of Nightvale Academy Mizar level, specialty Hunter. For Hayden, he doesn't remember his parents much so his Uncle and Aunt were much more like parents to him, and losing them hurt him deeply. He vowed he wouldn't allow his family to follow this seemingly cursed path of leaving children too early. Deep down he knows it wasn't in their control, they didn't abandon him by choice, however it doesn't change how he hurts.

### Sienna Seralien

Lady Sienna Seralien, currently 16 years old, she is expected to join her cousins and brother at Nightvale Academy in the upcoming year. Despite her lackluster academics, she is quite strong in spirit, mind, and body. She kept herself from falling into despair and grief for her Uncle and Aunt by meditating in the temple, she spent days at the alter, praying to Atia for their soul's safe travel. She left many offerings to her as well, outside of that she worked around to temple and estate to help keep it clean and in working order.

### Adler Seralien

Lord Adler Seralien, currently 15 years old, he spends most of his time working in the estate rather than the temple. He had promised his cousin Penelope he would look after all the creatures of the estate and that come by the temple. Despite being one of the youngest, he works diligently and trains equally as hard, having gained the motivation from watching his sister Sienna work so hard. Many think Adler is his cousins sibling as his skin tone and hair match Corvin's, however he always corrects them to the embarrassment of those that asked.
